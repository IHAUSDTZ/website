The Integrated Hill Areas Upliftment Society (IHAUS) is a non-governmental organization committed to the holistic economic and social development of the communities in Noney District, Manipur. Since its establishment, IHAUS has been working tirelessly to promote welfare, empowerment, and sustainable development in the hill areas.

vision

To build a just, inclusive, and self-reliant society in the hill areas of Manipur where every individual enjoys equal opportunities for growth, development, and well-being.

Mission

To empower marginalized communities through sustainable livelihood promotion, quality education, improved healthcare, environmental conservation, and capacity building, with a focus on participatory and inclusive development.

Key Area of Focus

    1. Rural Livelihood and Skill Development
    2. Education and Child Development
    3. Healthcare and Sanitation
    4. Women and Youth Empowerment
    5. Environmental Conservation and Natural Resource Management
    6. Disaster Preparedness and Relief

The Integrated Hill Area Upliftment Society (IHAUS) is committed to fostering inclusive rural development through a variety of initiatives. It supports sustainable livelihoods by promoting both traditional and modern practices, complemented by entrepreneurship and vocational training programs. In the education sector, IHAUS strengthens community-based schooling, offers scholarships, and raises awareness of child rights. Healthcare and sanitation are advanced through mobile medical camps, health campaigns, and WASH (Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene) initiatives to improve community well-being.

IHAUS actively empowers women and youth through the formation of self-help groups, access to microfinance, and leadership development workshops. Environmental conservation is also a priority, with programs focused on watershed management, afforestation, and the promotion of climate-resilient practices. To ensure community safety, IHAUS conducts disaster preparedness training and delivers emergency relief during natural calamities, enhancing local resilience and response capacity.
