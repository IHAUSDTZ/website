Our organization

The Integrated Hill Areas Upliftment Society (IHAUS) is a non-governmental organization committed to the holistic economic and social development of the communities in Noney District, Manipur. Since its establishment, IHAUS has been working tirelessly to promote welfare, empowerment, and sustainable development in the hill areas.

Mission

To empower marginalized communities through sustainable livelihood promotion, quality education, improved healthcare, environmental conservation, and capacity building, with a focus on participatory and inclusive development.

Vision

To build a just, inclusive, and self-reliant society in the hill areas of Manipur where every individual enjoys equal opportunities for growth, development, and well-being.

Key Areas Of Focus

  1. Rural Livelihood and Skill Development
  2. Education and Child Development
  3. Healthcare and Sanitation
  4. Women and Youth Empowerment
  5. Environmental Conservation and Natural Resource Management
  6. Disaster Preparedness and Relief

Some of the work done

Village level Geo-economic Development through Sustainable farming

Score of farmers huddled with experts for a minute deliberation on the topic, "Village level Geo-economic Development through Sustainable farming". The farmers meet was held on 28th April,2025 at the community Hall,Khamlang village,Kamjong district.A good number of farmers attended the meet which began at 11am and lasted for four hours.It was organized and sponsored by Tribal Resource Development Centre in collaboration with Development for rural people supported by Tribal Taste.It was a day long consultative meet where the farmers fraternity shared their ideas,expertise and experiences with one another.

April 28, 2025
Khamlang Village, Kamjong District

A One-Day Farmers’ Consultation Program (Phase-2)

A One-Day Farmers’ Consultation Program (Phase-2) was successfully conducted on 16th April 2025 at Shingkap Community Hall, Kamjong District, Manipur. The program focused on village-level geo-economic development through sustainable farming practices. Shri Athuii Panmei delivered an insightful speech on the health benefits of honey and the potential of beekeeping as a sustainable and profitable profession in Manipur.

April 16, 2025
Shingkap Community Hall, Kamjong District
